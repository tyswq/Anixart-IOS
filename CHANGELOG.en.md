# Changelog

Russian version: [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).
Format based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [1.0] — 2026-11-23

First stable release.

### Added
- **Red** theme: black with a red glow and glass panels.

### Fixed
- Comment input layout; the tab bar is hidden on that screen.
- Player lock button appears on tap and fades out with the other controls.

## [0.96] — BETA — 2026-11-22

### Added
- Continue watching on the home screen, with a progress bar.
- Search filters (genres, category, status, season, country, years, age rating, sort), search history and suggestions.
- Comments: write, reply, upvote/downvote.
- Star rating for titles.
- Profile pie chart; tapping a number opens that list.
- Player: 5-second next-episode countdown, Picture in Picture.

## [0.95] — BETA — 2026-11-22
